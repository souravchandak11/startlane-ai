# AEO Specialist: Screening Scorecard (INTERNAL, don't share with candidates)

Use this to score each completed questionnaire **before** booking a call. Goal: only meet the top 3 to 5 candidates.

---

## Step 1: Knockout check (any "No" means don't book a meeting)

| # | Check | Pass if… |
|---|-------|----------|
| K1 | Budget fit (Q7–Q8) | Accepts ₹25,000–₹30,000/month. Expected number sits inside the range. |
| K2 | Capacity (Q9) | Can commit enough hours for the work (suggest 25+ hrs/week). |
| K3 | US overlap (Q11) | "Yes" or a realistic "Sometimes" for a weekly call. |
| K4 | No conflict (Q12) | Not doing AEO/SEO for a competing youth soccer / camp brand. |
| K5 | Real AEO work (Q14–Q16) | Has done AEO/GEO work themselves **and** gives at least one piece of real proof. |
| K6 | Did Section F (Q34–Q39) | Actually ran prompts and looked at the ESU site. Didn't skip or give generic filler. |
| K7 | Own words | Answers aren't obviously pasted from ChatGPT (same structure for every answer, no specifics, buzzword-heavy). |

---

## Step 2: Weighted score (out of 100)

Score each area 1–5, then multiply by its weight.

| Area | Questions | Weight | What a **5** looks like | What a **1** looks like |
|------|-----------|:------:|-------------------------|-------------------------|
| Proven AEO results | Q15, Q16, Q18 | ×5 (25) | Named metrics (citations gained, AI share of voice, AI referral sessions/conversions), clear timeline, verifiable proof or reference. Honest about a failure. | "Increased visibility significantly." No numbers, no screenshots. |
| AEO knowledge depth | Q20–Q28 | ×4 (20) | Explains how each engine sources answers differently; concrete on-page tactics (answer-first copy, question headings, tables, original data); sensible schema choices; nuanced view on llms.txt and crawler access; understands entities. | Recycles SEO basics ("add keywords, build backlinks") or claims guaranteed rankings in ChatGPT. |
| ESU scenario quality | Q34–Q39 | ×4 (20) | Realistic parent prompts (age, city, cost, "is it worth it"). Real findings with screenshots. Gaps are specific to ESU's site. 30-day plan is concrete and doable. Realistic 90-day / 6-month targets. Thinks about seasonality. | Generic prompts, didn't run them, gaps could apply to any site, promises "#1 in ChatGPT in 30 days". |
| Local + technical SEO | Q17, Q23, Q33 | ×3 (15) | Has handled multi-location GBP and location pages. Can implement schema and fix technical issues without a dev. | Needs a developer for everything, never touched GBP. |
| Measurement & reporting | Q29–Q32 | ×2 (10) | Uses an AI visibility tool or a disciplined manual prompt tracker. Knows how to set up a GA4 channel group / regex for AI referrers. Ties KPIs to registrations. | Only reports rankings and traffic. Can't explain AI referral tracking. |
| Communication & fit | Q40–Q43, overall writing | ×2 (10) | Clear, concise, owns mistakes, proactive update rhythm, follows AI search news actively. | Vague, defensive, slow, or hard to follow. |

**Total = sum of (score × weight).** Max = 100.

### Decision

| Score | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **75–100** | Book a call this week. Priority candidate. |
| **60–74** | Book a call if the top pool is thin. Note the weak area to probe. |
| **Below 60** | Polite decline. |

---

## Green flags ✅

- Shows AI citations they personally earned, with dates.
- Has GA4 data showing sessions from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com.
- Talks about **share of voice across a prompt set**, not a single "ranking".
- Mentions that results vary by engine, location and session, and reports trends rather than snapshots.
- Brings up reviews, GBP, Reddit/community presence and third-party mentions without being prompted.
- Understands seasonality and plans content ahead of registration windows.
- Asks smart questions about ESU's locations, programs, and conversion tracking.

## Red flags 🚩

- Guarantees "#1 in ChatGPT" or fixed results in a short time.
- Suggests spammy tactics: fake reviews, mass AI-generated pages, astroturfing Reddit, buying links.
- Treats AEO as just "add FAQ schema".
- No proof at all, or proof that can't be verified.
- Section F answers are generic or clearly not researched.
- Expected pay outside the ₹25k–₹30k range, or tries to renegotiate the range before a call.
- Every answer has the same AI-generated shape (headings, bullet lists, no specifics).

---

## Step 3: Questions to probe on the call

Use these on the 30-minute call to check the questionnaire answers hold up.

1. "Walk me through your best AEO result. Share your screen and show me the tracking."
2. "You said [X] for ESU's biggest gap. How exactly would you fix it, step by step?"
3. "A parent asks Perplexity for 'the best soccer camps in New Jersey'. ESU isn't cited. What do you check first?"
4. "If AI visibility goes up but registrations don't, what do you do?"
5. "What would you need from us (access, content, people) in week 1?"
6. "What would make you leave a client engagement?"

---

## Optional Step 4: Paid trial task (for the final 1–2 candidates)

Before making an offer, consider a short **paid** trial (e.g. ₹3,000–₹5,000, 1 week):

- Build a 25-prompt tracking set for ESU across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode.
- Record baseline mentions and citations.
- Rewrite one camp page in answer-first format with valid schema.
- Deliver a one-page plan for the first 90 days.

Score it on accuracy, initiative, and how clearly they explain the work.
