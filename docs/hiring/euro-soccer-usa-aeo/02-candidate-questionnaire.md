# AEO Specialist: Candidate Questionnaire (Euro Soccer USA)

> **Send this to candidates before booking a call.** Paste it into a Google Form, Typeform, Tally, or a Google Doc.
> The internal scoring guide is in `03-screening-scorecard.md`. Don't send that file to candidates.

---

Thanks for your interest in the AEO Specialist role with Euro Soccer USA.

Before we set up a call, we'd like to learn how you think and what you've actually done. Please answer in your own words. We read every response closely, and short, specific answers beat long, generic ones. Answers that read as copied from an AI tool will not move forward.

**Time needed:** about 45 to 60 minutes (Section F is the longest part).
**Deadline:** please reply within 5 days of receiving this.

---

## Section A: The basics

1. Full name
2. Email and phone / WhatsApp
3. LinkedIn profile URL
4. Portfolio, website, or case-study link
5. City and time zone
6. How did you hear about this role?

## Section B: Fit and logistics

7. This role pays **₹25,000 to ₹30,000 per month**. Does that work for you? *(Yes / No / Only at the top of the range)*
8. What is your expected monthly compensation within that range, and why?
9. Is this full-time for you, or alongside other clients or a job? How many hours per week can you commit?
10. When can you start?
11. Can you join a weekly call that overlaps with US Eastern time (e.g. 7 to 9 PM IST / 9:30 to 11:30 AM ET)? *(Yes / No / Sometimes)*
12. Are you currently working on AEO or SEO for any youth sports, camps, education, or kids' activity brands? *(We ask to avoid conflicts of interest.)*
13. Are you comfortable signing an NDA and working inside our Google Workspace, Search Console, and GA4?

## Section C: Experience and proof

14. How many years have you worked in SEO overall, and how long have you done AEO / GEO / AI-search work specifically?
15. Describe **one AEO project** from start to finish:
    - What was the brand and industry? (Anonymise if needed.)
    - Where did they stand in AI answers when you started?
    - What exactly did you change?
    - What were the measurable results, and over what time frame?
16. Share **proof** for the project above: screenshots of AI citations, a visibility-tracking report, GA4 referral data from chatgpt.com / perplexity.ai / gemini.google.com, or a client reference we can contact.
17. Have you done **local SEO** for a business with several physical locations? Tell us how you handled Google Business Profiles and location pages.
18. Tell us about an AEO or SEO effort that **didn't** work. What did you learn and what would you do differently?
19. Which kinds of businesses have you worked with? *(Tick all that apply: local service, e-commerce, SaaS, education, sports / fitness, events, healthcare, other.)*

## Section D: AEO knowledge

20. In your own words, how is AEO different from traditional SEO? What stays the same?
21. How do ChatGPT (with search), Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews / AI Mode **decide which sources to cite**? Where do they differ?
22. What on-page changes make content more likely to be quoted in an AI answer? Give 3 to 5 concrete examples.
23. Which **schema.org types** would you implement for a youth soccer camp business, and why? Mention anything you'd specifically avoid.
24. What off-site signals matter most for AI visibility for a local, family-focused brand? Where would you start?
25. How do you handle **Reddit, Quora, and parent forums** as part of an AEO strategy without being spammy or breaking community rules?
26. What's your take on `llms.txt`, AI crawler access (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended) in `robots.txt`, and server-side rendering for AI crawlers?
27. How do you build a brand's **entity** (knowledge panel, Wikidata, consistent NAP, "sameAs" links), and why does it matter for AEO?
28. AI answers change week to week and differ by user. How do you deal with that volatility when you plan and report?

## Section E: Tools, measurement and reporting

29. Which tools have you used for AI visibility tracking? *(e.g. Profound, Peec AI, Otterly.ai, Semrush AI Toolkit, Ahrefs Brand Radar, Scrunch, manual prompt tracking, other.)* Which do you prefer and why?
30. Which SEO tools do you use day to day? *(Search Console, GA4, Ahrefs, Semrush, Screaming Frog, Sitebulb, Schema validator, Looker Studio, other.)*
31. What **KPIs** would you report to us every month? How would you connect AI visibility to actual camp registrations?
32. How would you track traffic and conversions that come from AI assistants in GA4?
33. How comfortable are you editing a website yourself (WordPress / Webflow / Shopify / HTML)? Can you add schema and fix technical issues without a developer?

## Section F: Euro Soccer USA scenario (practical)

> Please do the research yourself. Spend no more than 30 to 40 minutes on this section. We care about your thinking, not polish.

34. Pick **five prompts** a parent in the US might type into ChatGPT or Perplexity when looking for a soccer camp or clinic for their child. Write them out exactly as a parent would.
35. Run at least **two** of those prompts in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Mode. Who gets mentioned or cited? Is Euro Soccer USA there? Share screenshots or links if you can.
36. Look at the Euro Soccer USA website. Name the **three biggest AEO gaps** you see, and how you'd fix each one.
37. What would your **first 30 days** look like? List what you'd deliver in week 1, weeks 2 to 3, and week 4.
38. What results would you consider realistic at **90 days** and at **6 months**? Be honest. We prefer realistic over impressive.
39. Camps are seasonal. How would you time content and AEO work around registration windows (spring and summer camps, holiday clinics)?

## Section G: Working style

40. How do you prefer to communicate updates: async (Slack, Loom, email), calls, or a mix? How often?
41. Tell us about a time you disagreed with a client or manager about strategy. How did it play out?
42. How do you keep up with changes in AI search? Name two or three sources you actually follow.
43. Anything else you'd like us to know?

---

**What happens next:** we review every questionnaire within 3 to 4 working days. If there's a fit, we'll reach out to book a 30-minute call where we'll go through your answers and your Section F findings together.

Thank you for your time.
