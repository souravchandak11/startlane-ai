# Outreach Messages

> Never use the client's name in messages before the NDA is signed.

## 1. Sending the Round 1 questionnaire

> Hi {First name}, thanks for your interest in the AEO Specialist role.
>
> You'd be working on a youth soccer camp and program provider in the US. We'll share the brand name with finalists under NDA.
>
> Before we book a call, we ask every candidate to complete a short questionnaire (about 60 minutes, including a quick hands-on scan of AI search results in a US city): {questionnaire link}
>
> Please send it back within 5 days. The role pays ₹25,000 to ₹30,000 per month, depending on experience and proven results.

## 2. Reminder (day 4, if no response)

> Hi {First name}, a quick reminder about the AEO questionnaire: {questionnaire link}. We're closing this round on {date}. If the timing doesn't work, just let me know.

## 3. Inviting to the screening call

> Hi {First name}, thanks for the thoughtful answers, especially {one specific thing they said}. We'd like to set up a 30-minute call to go through your responses and your market scan. Please pick a slot here: {calendar link}

## 4. Inviting to Round 2 (after the screening call)

> Hi {First name}, great speaking with you. We'd like to move you to the final round.
>
> Next step is a short paid mini-audit (about 4 to 5 hours, ₹{amount} paid on delivery). Before we share the brand and the brief, please sign the attached NDA: {NDA link}
>
> Once it's signed, I'll send the brief right away.

## 5. Polite decline

> Hi {First name}, thank you for the time you put into this. We've decided to move forward with candidates whose experience is a closer match for this role right now. We appreciate your interest and wish you the best.
