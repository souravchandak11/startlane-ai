## What to deliver

### 1. AI visibility baseline
- Build a set of **20 to 25 parent prompts** across at least 2 of our markets ({metro 1}, {metro 2}). Mix ages, levels, seasons and decision stages.
- Run them in **ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode / AI Overviews**.
- Record whether {Client name} is mentioned, whether it's cited (with a link), its position, and which competitors and sources appear.
- Share the sheet.

### 2. Competitor and source analysis
- Which **3 to 5 competitors** appear most often, and why do you think they win?
- Which **third-party sources** (directories, articles, Reddit threads, review sites) keep getting cited? Where is {Client name} missing?

### 3. On-site review
Look at the homepage, one camp or program page, and one location page.
- The **5 biggest gaps** stopping AI engines from quoting or recommending these pages.
- A short **before/after rewrite** of one section in answer-first format.
- What structured data is there, what's broken or missing, and what you'd add.

### 4. Local and trust signals
- How {Client name} currently handles Google Business Profile, reviews, and venue/location pages, and what you'd change.
- Which trust signals (coach credentials, safety, ratios, policies, testimonials) are missing or hard to find.

### 5. 90-day plan
- Your **top 10 actions**, ranked by impact and effort, mapped to the camp registration calendar.
- The **KPIs** you'd report monthly and realistic targets for day 90.
- What you'd need from us (access, people, content, budget).
