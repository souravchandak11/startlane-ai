# AEO Specialist: Round 1 Questionnaire

> **Send this to shortlisted applicants before any call.** Paste it into Google Forms, Typeform or Tally.
> The client is described only as "a youth soccer camp and program provider in the US". Don't add the brand name, website or locations to this form.

---

Thanks for your interest in the AEO Specialist role.

You'll be working on a **youth soccer camp and program provider that runs camps and clinics across several US states**. We'll share the brand name with finalists after an NDA.

This questionnaire checks two things: whether you can do AEO, and whether you understand **how American parents choose soccer programs for their kids**. Answer in your own words. Short, specific answers beat long, generic ones. Answers that read as copied from an AI tool won't move forward.

**Time needed:** about 60 minutes (Section G is the hands-on part).
**Deadline:** within 5 days of receiving this.

---

## Section A: The basics

1. Full name
2. Email and phone / WhatsApp
3. LinkedIn profile URL
4. Portfolio or case-study link
5. City and time zone

## Section B: Fit and logistics

6. The role pays **₹25,000 to ₹30,000 per month**. Does that work for you? *(Yes / No / Only at the top of the range)*
7. Where in that range do you expect to be, and why?
8. Is this full-time for you, or alongside other work? How many hours per week can you commit?
9. When can you start?
10. Can you join a weekly call overlapping with US Eastern time (e.g. 7 to 9 PM IST)? *(Yes / No / Sometimes)*
11. Are you currently doing SEO or AEO for any youth sports, camp, or kids' activity business in the US? *(We ask to avoid conflicts of interest.)*
12. Are you willing to sign an NDA before Round 2?

## Section C: Experience and proof

13. Years in SEO overall, and years doing AEO / GEO / AI-search work specifically.
14. Describe **one AEO project** from start to finish: industry, where the brand stood in AI answers at the start, what you changed, and the measurable results over what time frame.
15. Share **proof** for that project: AI citation screenshots, a visibility-tracking report, GA4 data showing visits from chatgpt.com / perplexity.ai / gemini.google.com, or a client reference.
16. Have you worked with **US clients**? Which industries, and what did you learn about US customers that was different from other markets?
17. Have you done SEO or marketing for a **local or seasonal business** (camps, schools, tutoring, events, fitness, tourism)? What was the seasonality, and how did you plan around it?
18. Tell us about an SEO or AEO effort that **didn't** work. What happened and what did you change?

## Section D: The US youth soccer market

> No research tools needed for most of these. We want to see what you already understand.

19. In a typical US family, **who researches and books a youth soccer camp**, and what are their top 3 worries before paying? How would that shape the content you create?
20. Walk us through the **US camp calendar**. When do parents start researching summer camps, when do most register, and what other program seasons exist through the year? How would you schedule AEO and content work around this?
21. Name the **types of competitors** a youth soccer camp provider faces in the US, with a few real examples of each. Which do you think win AI answers today, and why?
22. Youth soccer camps often run at **schools, parks and club fields the business doesn't own**, and the venues change each season. How would you handle Google Business Profile, location pages and local AEO in that situation? What would you avoid?
23. Which **third-party websites, directories and communities** do US parents use and trust when choosing camps and kids' activities? Which of them tend to show up as sources in AI answers?
24. What **trust and safety signals** do US parents look for before sending their child to a camp? How would you make these visible to both parents and AI engines?
25. How do US youth soccer **age groups and levels** (U8, U10, recreational vs. travel/club, competitive pathways) change the questions parents ask? How would you structure content for a beginner camp versus an advanced or elite program?
26. Soccer is especially popular with **Hispanic and Latino families** in the US. Would you include Spanish-language content in an AEO strategy? How?
27. This business creates content about children and markets to parents. What **compliance or sensitivity issues** would you keep in mind (photos, reviews, data, claims)?

## Section E: AEO knowledge, applied to this market

28. In your own words, how is AEO different from traditional SEO, and what stays the same?
29. How do ChatGPT (with search), Perplexity and Google AI Overviews / AI Mode **choose which sources to cite**? Where do they differ?
30. A parent asks: *"Is a week-long soccer camp worth it for a 7-year-old?"* What would a page that wins this answer look like? Describe the structure, not just "add FAQs".
31. Which **schema.org types** would you use for camps that run on specific dates at changing venues? What would you avoid?
32. How would you build presence on **Reddit, Facebook parent groups and similar communities** without being spammy or breaking community rules?
33. What's your take on `llms.txt`, AI crawler access in `robots.txt` (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended), and JavaScript-heavy sites?
34. AI answers vary by engine, location and session, and change week to week. How do you measure and report progress honestly despite that?

## Section F: Tools and reporting

35. Which AI-visibility tools have you used (Profound, Peec AI, Otterly.ai, Semrush AI Toolkit, Ahrefs Brand Radar, manual prompt tracking, other)? Which do you prefer and why?
36. How would you track visits and registrations that come from AI assistants in GA4?
37. Which **monthly KPIs** would you report for a camp business, and how would you connect AI visibility to registrations?
38. Can you edit a website yourself (WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, HTML) and add schema without a developer?

## Section G: Hands-on market scan (30 to 40 minutes)

> Pick **one US metro area** (for example Dallas–Fort Worth, Atlanta, Northern New Jersey, Charlotte, or San Diego). You don't need to know who our client is.

39. Write **8 prompts** a parent in that metro might type into ChatGPT or Perplexity when looking for a soccer camp or program. Cover different ages, levels and stages of the decision (exploring, comparing, ready to book).
40. Run at least **3** of those prompts in **two different engines** (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode or Gemini). Which brands and which sources get cited? Share screenshots or links.
41. What do the brands and pages that **win** those answers have in common? What would a new brand need to do to appear alongside them?
42. If you joined tomorrow, what would your **first 30 days** look like for a camp brand in this market? Week 1, weeks 2 to 3, week 4.
43. What results are realistic at **90 days** and **6 months**? Be honest. We prefer realistic over impressive.

## Section H: Working style

44. How do you prefer to share updates (Slack, Loom, email, calls), and how often?
45. Tell us about a time you disagreed with a client about strategy. How did it play out?
46. Which two or three sources do you follow to keep up with AI search?
