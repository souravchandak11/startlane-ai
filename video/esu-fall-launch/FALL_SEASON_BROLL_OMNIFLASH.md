## OmniFlash B-roll prompts

**Ground rule:** the brand planner says the kids on screen are real ESU footage only. So these B-rolls never show a child's face: they're hands, feet, backs, objects and empty fields. They read as cinematic inserts, not as fake students, and they cut cleanly with the real clips. Kits are plain pink / lime-green pinnies with no print, to match the real footage without inventing a logo.

**Settings for all:** vertical 9:16 · 5 s · realistic / cinematic style · no audio needed (the SFX are already in the edit). Generate 2–3 takes and keep the one with natural hands and no faces.
**Posting:** if photorealistic AI clips go into the reel, switch on Instagram's **AI label** when posting (Meta asks for it on realistic AI video, and the synthetic voiceover arguably counts too).
