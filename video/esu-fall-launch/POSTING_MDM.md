## How the video is built to convert
| Time | Beat | Retention device |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.2 s | "parents of 1-year-olds 👀" call-out; a ball bounces in on the beat and the toddler kicks it at the camera | **Visual hook:** motion on frame 0, plus the audience named in text. **Audio hook:** ukulele groove with ball bounces |
| 1.2–3.2 s | Record-scratch freeze on "wait…", rewind, then "your 1-year-old can play SOCCER?" | Pattern interrupt plus an open question |
| 3.2–5 s | "yep!" stamp; Mom and Dad slide in for "you get to play too" | Answers the question and opens the parent angle |
| 5–13.5 s | **Pain:** the world turns grey: "same playground / same swings / same Saturday" stamps and a calendar of identical Saturdays; the toddler-energy meter overflows to 999% as the walls close in; then "they're only this little once" | Relatable boredom, then an emotional turn (the tiny hand holding a parent's finger) |
| 13.5–17 s | The calendar flips to **THIS WEEKEND**. An aim arc draws in, the toddler kicks… **GOAL!** Colour floods back, the music drops and confetti flies; "together." | The payoff lands exactly on the beat drop |
| 17–31 s | **Offer:** the Mommy, Daddy & Me Soccer lockup with the ESU crest; 30-minute timer, ages 12–24 months, "you" and "them" walking hand in hand; themed-game cards; balance, coordination and confidence badges; the "no pressure" gauge drops to 0; "play!" | One new visual every 1–2 s |
| 31–38.5 s | "one very good nap after" with a 5-star nap rating (a joke parents recognise), then milestone cards: first kick, first goal, first high five; "you'll be right there for all of it" | Emotion and a reason to save or share |
| 38.5–47 s | **CTA card:** Sat & Sun · Oct 3 – Nov 22 · 10:15–10:45 AM · ages 12–24 months · The Sports Park · "try a class · $34 drop-in" · **Book their first class →** with a tap animation · "Proudly sponsored by Wateria" | A soft, low-commitment ask |

The ESU crest (top-left) and the Wateria **Official Sponsor** badge (top-right) stay on screen throughout. All text stays between y ≈ 150 and 1500 px, so the Reels UI never covers it.
