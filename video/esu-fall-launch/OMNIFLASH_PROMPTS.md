# OmniFlash prompts: Euro Soccer USA Fall Academy (48 s = five 10 s clips)

Five prompts that generate the full reel as **live-action footage in the Metro Media House
style**, shot for shot on the same 48-second timeline as the Remotion edit. The voiceover,
captions, crest and end card go on top in the edit.

## What the reference reels look like (measured from @metromedia.house frames)
Eleven reels were pulled. Six were sampled every 0.25 s and at every scene change, then checked by eye.

| Trait | Measured | Written into the prompts as |
|---|---|---|
| Palette | About 90% neutrals: deep black `#07080a`, pale paper grey `#e5e2e6`, mid greys `#a3a19a`. Warm skin midtones `#614944`–`#745c54`. **One accent at a time**: red `#d12329`, pitch green `#2e6332` | "Near-black shadows, pale grey paper, desaturated neutrals, one saturated accent (red kit or green turf)" |
| Grade | Most shots black & white or heavily desaturated; colour is warm and red-leaning | Problem half **B&W**, solution half **warm golden-red colour** |
| Pace | Median shot 0.24–0.52 s. 10–18 cuts in the first 5 s. A big hero moment lands at 3.5–5.5 s | "Rapid-fire montage, a new shot every half second" in the hook. The hero shot lands at about 4.5 s |
| Camera | Slow push-ins on every shot (≈1.00→1.08), locked-off or gentle drift, no whip pans | "Every shot slowly pushes in" |
| Signature moves | **Pull-back reveal**: a full-bleed image shrinks into a small bordered photo print on pale grey paper. **Prints inside objects** (an open old book). **Floating clouds of photo prints** with blurred back rows. Glitch/flash frames between shots | Each is written as a physical, text-free action the model can render |
| Texture | 35 mm grain, soft halation, light vignette, low-key single-source light, hard shadows | "35 mm film grain, halation, low-key single-source light" |

## Rules baked into every prompt
- **No typography at all.** There is no on-screen wording in any prompt, and every prompt carries a negative line. Screens always face away or show only abstract glow. Kits, medals, balls and walls are plain.
- **Identical cast and world wording in every clip**, so the five generations match.
- **9:16 vertical.** Faces and action sit in the upper 60% of frame; the lower third is left calm for captions.
- **Audio:** ambient sound only. The voiceover and score come from the edit.
- **Clip 5:** generate 10 s and keep the first 8 s. The final 1.5 s of the reel is the crest card, added in the edit.
