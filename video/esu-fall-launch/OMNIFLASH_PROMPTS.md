# OmniFlash prompts: Euro Soccer USA Fall Academy (48 s, five 10 s clips)

These five prompts generate the **live-action footage layer** of the launch reel. They follow
the reel's exact timeline, so the voiceover, captions, logo and cards from the Remotion edit
(`src/compositions/ESU_FallLaunch.tsx`) drop straight on top.

**Rules baked into every prompt:**
- **Zero typography.** No text, letters, numbers, logos, signage, jersey numbers or readable screens. All wording is added in the edit.
- **Every screen faces away from the camera**, or shows only abstract coloured glow.
- **Characters and location are described identically every time**, so the five generations match. Keep the "CAST & WORLD" block word-for-word.
- **9:16 vertical.** Faces and action sit in the upper 60% of frame, because the lower third is reserved for captions.
- **Reference edit style (Metro Media House):**
  - Documentary-cinematic 35 mm look with fine grain and soft halation.
  - Slow push-ins on every shot.
  - A hard cut every 0.5–1.5 s.
  - The problem half is **high-contrast black & white with a choppy 12 fps stepped-motion feel**.
  - The solution half is **warm, red-leaning golden colour**.
  - No whip pans, no speed ramps, no dissolves.
- **Clip 5 runs 10 s. Trim it to 8 s**; the last 1.5 s of the reel is the logo card on black, added in the edit.

### How to assemble
1. Generate the five clips at 1080×1920. If OmniFlash caps a generation at 9 s, split each prompt at its midpoint timestamp; the timestamps are already on shot boundaries.
2. Drop the clips into `public/footage/clip1.mp4` … `clip5.mp4` and place them under the existing shots. Keep the Remotion typography, cards, logo, voiceover and music on top, because OmniFlash output never contains any wording.
3. Grade check: 5.6–19.2 s must be black & white, and everything from the kick at 19.2 s onward warm colour. This colour flip is the emotional turn of the reel.
